## The Navras Score
A Navras Score is given by hand, out of 100, by CineRaaga's editor after
watching the film. It is never calculated from votes, box office or other
critics. A film we have not reviewed has no Navras Score.

## What the numbers mean
- 85 and above: Must watch
- 70 to 84: Worth watching
- Below 70: Mixed — your call

## Other numbers you may see
Film cards and film pages show an audience rating taken from TMDb's user votes.
That is not a Navras Score.

## Our lists
Every list is ranked by hand. A film's position is an editorial choice.
